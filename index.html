01 About Me

I live between two worlds—the discipline of engineering, and the intuition of art.

I hold a degree in Information & Communications Engineering, and for as long as I can remember I've been trying to find the balance between the two halves of who I am: the engineer who thinks in precision and structure, and the artist who thinks in color, texture, and form.

Between security labs and sketchbooks, between client dashboards and hand-sewn pieces, I've learned that discipline and creativity aren't opposites — they sharpen each other. That balance is what I bring to every project I take on, whether it's technical or entirely handmade.
